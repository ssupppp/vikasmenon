Puff Hunt

Move using face, shoot using eyes

Play it on itch.io

Turn your head to aim, blink to pop. Puffbirds drift across a meadow and you shoot them down with your face, no mouse or keyboard needed. Pip the fox cheers when you hit one and laughs when you miss. Five rounds, each one faster than the last.

The first time you play, a short setup under the night sky teaches the controls one at a time and maps how far your head actually turns. After that the game remembers you and drops you straight into the birds.

Face tracking runs in the browser with MediaPipe. The webcam video never leaves your machine. Needs a laptop or desktop with a camera.
