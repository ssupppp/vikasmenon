Puff Hunt

Move using face, shoot using eyes

Play it on itch.io

I've been playing shooting games for the better part of my life. One idea kept coming back: what if you could aim with something other than a mouse?

Sometimes you imagine a thing and leave it at that. Sometimes you have to find out if it's actually fun.

The plan was to try it in CS2, but I didn't want to risk a VAC ban, so I went with something easier. Inspired by Duck Hunt, here's Puff Hunt. Move your face to aim, blink to shoot.

Face tracking runs in the browser. The webcam video never leaves your machine. Needs a laptop or desktop with a camera.
