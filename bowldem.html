Bowldem

Daily Cricket Puzzle Game

Wordle, but for cricket

Bowldem gives you six tries to guess a mystery cricketer. Each guess reveals clues about nationality, playing role, batting and bowling style, IPL team, and career stats. A new puzzle drops every day.

There's a nostalgia layer built in — clues nudge you through eras, teams, and names you might not have thought about in years, so each puzzle doubles as a small trip back through cricket history. Curated database of 500+ international and IPL cricketers.

The daily newsletter

Subscribers get an email every day at stumps with the answer to that day's puzzle and the name of the previous day's winner.
